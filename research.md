## 🏆 Competitive Landscape

### Direct Competitors
1. **Bilt (Receipt Management)**
   - Strengths: Simple receipt scanning
   - Weaknesses: No warranty focus, limited features
   - Market Share: ~2% of receipt apps

2. **Everdence (Digital Receipts)**
   - Strengths: Merchant partnerships
   - Weaknesses: No AI extraction, basic tracking
   - Market Share: ~1% of receipt apps

3. **Sortly (Inventory Management)**
   - Strengths: Visual organization
   - Weaknesses: No warranty focus, manual entry

4. **Proactive Intelligence**: Smart reminders vs passive storage
